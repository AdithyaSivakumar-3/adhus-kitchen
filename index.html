The cook behind the kitchen

I'm Adithya — engineer by profession, cook by pure joy. This site is three years of evenings spent stirring kuzhambus and gravies the way they're made back home, folding modaks for Ganesh Chaturthi, and occasionally letting a lasagna or ramen bowl crash the party. Every photo here is a dish I cooked and plated myself.

Feeling hungry scrolling through? Come over — I'll cook it for you, served with extra love ♥
